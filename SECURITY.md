# Security

Lyng takes the privacy of your data seriously. When you connect email accounts to the app, your email credentials are stored securely in your system keychain. Lyng does not transmit, store or process your mail in the cloud, and there is no vendor account: features like open and link tracking talk to a self-hosted server that you configure yourself.

## Responsible Disclosure

If you have reason to believe that Lyng is subject to a security vulnerability or breach, please report it through GitHub's [private security advisory](https://github.com/Bojanni050/lyng/security/advisories/new) for this repository, with as much information as you can provide. You can expect a response within a few days.

Vulnerabilities that originate in the upstream [Mailspring](https://github.com/Foundry376/Mailspring) codebase are best reported to the Mailspring project as well, so they can fix them for their users too.
